# Fullscreen streak

After a newly saved lesson reaches the daily goal, “Verder” opens a separate streak screen. The flame ignites, the real streak number rolls in, then the calendar and actions appear. No avatar or third-party animation assets.

Verified at 320×640, 390×844 and 1280×800: no overflow, accessible heading, keyboard focus, next lesson and ghost return button. Reduced motion shows the final screen immediately. Returning, reloading and background rendering never duplicate XP or replay the celebration.

Validation: 286 tests, standard and static builds pass. Browser recording: `artifacts/streak-completion/streak-animation.gif`.
