# Course coverage

The anatomy and biomechanics curriculum includes 1084 course questions. A separately labelled coaching supplement extends practice beyond that document. This checklist concerns its teachable content, rather than a separate exam syllabus.

Three agents audited basis, muscle/exercise tables, and patterns/programming, then cross-reviewed each other. Visual length-profile bars were also checked against rendered PDF pages.

Tests reject missing question references, uncovered questions, invalid course page numbers and incomplete exercise/profile inventories. The private original PDF and extracted pages stay outside the repository. A searchable question directory at `#vragen` makes every question available without unlocking levels.

## Coaching supplement

The 41 additional questions cover external versus internal focus, analogy cues, their overlap, testing one cue, adapting an exercise and checking retention without help. Thirty-five applications cover every listed pattern leak plus dead bug, bird dog, plank, back extension hold, Pallof press, anti-rotation chop, side plank and KB march & walk. Each links a leak to a cue, an observable check and an adjustment if control is lost.

Source labels distinguish original coaching applications from course facts. Related PDF pages describe the underlying leaks; research references support the learning principles, not each individual correction. Natural upward scapular rotation and slight elevation during an overhead press remain explicit. The old chapter lessons retain their question partitions and review fillers; new material starts in additional lessons. Legacy progress never marks this supplement complete.

Principle references: [Grgic et al. (2021), attentional focus and strength](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34822352/) and [Zacks & Friedman (2020), analogy learning](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-020-63999-1). The applications are newly written and released with the question bank under CC BY-SA 4.0. No source passages or third-party component code are copied.
